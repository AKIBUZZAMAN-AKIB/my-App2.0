Rights and privacy

OpenShelf links to the original provider and does not host, copy or proxy any book. Check the licence, regional access and reuse terms shown by the provider before sharing a file. Saved books, notes, reading progress and keys never leave this device.

Readers load pinned libraries (PDF.js, pdf-lib, EPUB.js, JSZip) from cdn.jsdelivr.net the first time you use them, so they need an internet connection.
